# Watch & Read: Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 3, 2026_

Watch & Read is a browser extension that keeps videos playing in a floating window while you read the comments on Facebook and Instagram.

**No data collection.** Watch & Read does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal or browsing data. It has no servers, no analytics, no accounts and no third-party services.

**Page access.** The extension runs only on facebook.com and instagram.com, plus the current tab when you press its keyboard shortcut or the button in its popup. On those pages it only finds video elements and moves them into the browser's picture-in-picture window. It does not read your posts, messages, comments or account information.

**Settings.** Your three on/off preferences are saved with the browser's extension storage. If you use browser sync, the browser may sync them between your own devices. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.

**Contact.** Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/muamerarifi-pixel/watch-and-read/issues
