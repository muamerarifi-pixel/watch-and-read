# Watch & Read

Keep Facebook and Instagram videos playing while you read the comments.

When you open the comments under a playing video, the video moves into a small floating window and keeps playing. You can move and resize the window anywhere while you scroll and read.

## Download

**[Download watch-and-read.zip](https://github.com/muamerarifi-pixel/watch-and-read/releases/latest/download/watch-and-read.zip)**

## Install (Chrome, Edge, Brave)

1. Download the zip above and unzip it. You get a folder named `watch-and-read`.
2. Move that folder somewhere permanent (for example `Documents/Extensions/watch-and-read`). Chrome loads the extension from this folder, so don't delete it.
3. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
4. Click **Load unpacked** and select the `watch-and-read` folder (the one that contains `manifest.json`).
5. Pin the extension (puzzle icon → pin), then reload any open Facebook or Instagram tabs.

## How it works

- **Auto-float:** click the Comment button under a playing video as usual. The video jumps into a floating window and keeps playing while the comments open. The duplicate copy Facebook opens in the post window is muted.
- **Hover button:** hover over any video and a **Float & keep playing** button appears in its top-left corner.
- **Shortcut:** `Alt+Shift+P` floats the playing video or brings it back. It works on any website. You can change it at `chrome://extensions/shortcuts`.
- **Back to normal:** click **Back to page**, or close the floating window.
- The toolbar popup has a switch for each feature.

## Update

Download the zip again, replace the files in your folder, then click the reload ↻ icon on the extension's card in `chrome://extensions`.

## Privacy

Watch & Read collects no data and sends nothing anywhere. It only runs on facebook.com and instagram.com, plus the current tab when you use the shortcut or the popup button.
